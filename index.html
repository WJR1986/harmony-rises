About Hayley

A compassionate, person-centred approach

Hi, I’m Hayley, the heart behind Harmony Rises Counselling.

I offer a safe, supportive space where you can come exactly as you are—without judgement, pressure, or expectation. I understand that reaching out for support isn’t always easy, and I deeply respect the courage it takes to begin this journey.

My approach is compassionate, down-to-earth, and centred around you. I work from a person-centred, humanistic perspective, meaning I believe you are the expert of your own life.

I also work integratively, drawing from approaches such as psychodynamic therapy and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), adapting our sessions to suit your individual needs.

Whether you're navigating anxiety, low mood, past experiences, self-worth, or simply feeling stuck, you don’t have to face it alone. I’m here to walk alongside you at your own pace.

Confidentiality & Privacy

Your privacy is extremely important. All sessions and communications are treated with strict confidentiality in accordance with professional ethical guidelines.

Information shared will not be disclosed without your consent, except in rare circumstances where there is a legal or ethical obligation to do so (e.g. risk of harm to yourself or others).

Any personal data provided via email or contact will be stored securely and used solely for communication regarding counselling services.

By getting in touch, you consent to being contacted in response to your enquiry.

If you have any questions about how your data is handled, please feel free to ask.
